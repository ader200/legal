Privacy & Data Protection

How OpusBot collects, uses, and protects your personal information

Last Updated

§1 Information We Collect

OpusBot collects only the minimum information necessary for the service to function:

Email Address

Obtained through Google OAuth during login. Used exclusively to identify your account.

API Tokens

Access credentials for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. Stored encrypted (AES-256) and never transmitted in plain text.

Post Metadata

Titles, descriptions, scheduling dates, and post status. Stored for calendar functionality.

Multimedia Files

Videos uploaded by the user for publishing. Stored temporarily and deleted after publication.

§2 How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • 01 Publish content — Send videos to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook on your behalf, under your direct control.
  • 02 Schedule posts — Execute automatic publications according to the dates and times you configure.
  • 03 Manage connections — Maintain active connections with your social media accounts.
  • 04 Show statistics — Display the status of your posts (published, pending, failed).

We DO NOT use your information for:

Targeted advertising, marketing, data selling, profiling, or any purpose other than described above.

§3 Storage and Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • → AES-256 Encryption — All API tokens are stored encrypted. They are never saved in plain text.
  • → Own Server — Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by OpusBot, not on third-party services.
  • → Restricted Access — Only the owner user can access their data. Administrators do not have access to tokens.
  • → Secure Transfer — All communication is performed via HTTPS/TLS.

§4 Sharing Data with Third Parties

OpusBot DOES NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

The only cases where data is shared are:

  • → With social networks — TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook receive only the content you authorize to publish.
  • → By legal obligation — If a judicial authority requests information through a valid legal order.

Legal Protection — Intermediary

OpusBot acts exclusively as a technical intermediary between the user and social media platforms. The user is solely responsible for the content they publish. OpusBot is NOT responsible for how each user uses the service. We reserve the right to permanently ban any account that uses the platform to:

  • • Publish illegal, violent, discriminatory content or content that infringes on third-party rights.
  • • Perform spam or unauthorized mass publishing.
  • • Violate the terms of service of connected social networks.
  • • Attempt to improperly access other users' data.

§6 Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can exercise the following rights at any time:

Disconnect Accounts

You can disconnect any of your social networks from the app settings. This immediately removes the access token.

Delete Account

You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us via email.

Export Data

You can request a copy of your current data (what exists in the system at that time) via email at godsgome@gmail.com. Already deleted data is not available.

Rectify Information

You can update or correct your personal information at any time from your profile.

§7 Data Retention

OpusBot retains data under the following conditions:

  • 01 API Tokens: Retained while the account is active to allow automatic reconnection with social networks. They are deleted when the user deletes their account or when an administrator manually removes the user to free up space.
  • 02 Videos and multimedia files: Already published videos are not automatically deleted. Videos pending publication are retained until the user publishes or manually deletes them.
  • 03 Post metadata: Retained while the account is active. If a user is deleted, existing videos may have publishing issues if social media accounts are not manually reassigned before deletion.
  • 04 Account deletion: When you request account deletion, OpusBot will remove your API tokens and associated data. Previously published videos on social media will NOT be affected (they are already on the platforms). You will be informed before proceeding if dependencies exist.

§8 Cookies and Tracking

OpusBot DOES NOT use tracking cookies, targeted advertising, or tracking technologies.

The only cookies used are those strictly necessary for user session functionality (authentication).

§9 Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy at any time. The policies are permanently available on this page so you can review them whenever you wish.

When significant changes are made, you will be notified when you log in to the platform. You must accept the updated policy before continuing to use the service. By accepting, you confirm that you have read and understood the changes made.

At Any Time

You can review this policy directly from the "Privacy" and "Terms" links available on the platform and in the footer.
